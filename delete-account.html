Delete your Lumi account and data

Lumi: Pregnancy & Postpartum Pilates, by KRM Studio

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Lumi and tap Me.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Under "Privacy and data", tap Delete my data and confirm.

This deletes your account right away, with your profile (stage, delivery type, dates, language), your class history and your email or Google sign-in link. It also clears everything stored on the phone.

Without the app

Email supportkrmstudio@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my Lumi account". We delete the account within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is kept

Nothing from your Lumi account is kept after deletion. Google Play and RevenueCat keep purchase records as the law requires for payments. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.
