Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 October 2026 (invite codes, reminders)

In short: Lumi works without an account. We never ask for your weight and never show BMI. How you feel after a class, your body limits and your daily check-in stay on your phone. Your health information is never used for advertising. You can delete everything from the app at any time.

1. Who we are

Lumi: Pregnancy & Postpartum Pilates ("Lumi", "we") is made by KRM Studio. Contact: supportkrmstudio@gmail.com. We reply within 2 working days.

2. What stays only on your phone

These are never sent to our servers:

  • How you felt after each class, and your daily check-in (tired, okay, good)
  • "My limits" (positions you cannot do)
  • Your first name, if you add one
  • Challenge progress and rest-day marks, rest time, language and animation settings
  • Reminder settings. Reminders are local notifications scheduled by the app on your phone

4. Services that process data for us

  • Supabase (database and sign-in): stores the data in section 3.
  • RevenueCat (subscriptions): receives your random account ID and your purchase and subscription status from Google Play. RevenueCat never receives your health information.
  • Resend (email delivery): only if you send a gift by email; receives the recipient's email address and the gift code.
  • Google Play (payments): handles payment. We never see your card details.
  • Google Sign-In: only if you choose it; Google shares your email and account ID with us.
  • Google AdMob (ads in the free version): shows ads only between classes, never during exercise. We request non-personalised ads with a family-safe content rating. AdMob may still collect device information such as IP address and advertising ID to deliver ads, count views and prevent fraud. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland we ask for consent with Google's consent form before ads load. Premium has no ads. See how Google uses information from apps.

These providers may process data outside your country. They act on our instructions and protect it under their own security and privacy terms.

5. What we never collect

Weight, BMI, body measurements, photos, contacts, precise location, or the content of your messages.

6. How long we keep data

We keep server data while you use Lumi. If you delete your account, we delete your account, profile and class history right away. Purchase records stay with Google Play and RevenueCat as the law requires for payments.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete everything: in the app go to Me → Settings → Delete my data. This deletes your account and server data, and clears the phone. See Delete your account.
  • Use Lumi without an account: signing in is optional.
  • Ads: choose Premium for no ads, or change your consent in the app or in your device settings.
  • You may ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it by emailing supportkrmstudio@gmail.com. Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may also complain to your data protection authority.

8. Health and safety

Lumi offers gentle, general exercise guidance. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat anything. Always get your doctor's or midwife's OK before exercising during pregnancy or after birth.

9. Children

Lumi is for adults. It is not directed at children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

10. Security

Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Database rules let each account read only its own data.

11. Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
